## Who we are

- **Name:** Pujo Parikrama 2026
- **Handle:** @pujoparikrama.guide
- **What we do, in one sentence:** A free Kolkata Durga Puja pandal-hopping guide: area-wise walking routes from
  the nearest Metro station, and live quiet hours for every pandal, at https://pujoparikramaguide.in/
- **Who we are talking to:** Kolkata and Bengal pujo-goers aged 18 to 35 planning their nights for 17 to 21 October
  2026, plus Bengalis outside Kolkata who follow pujo from afar. They speak Bengali at home and read English on
  their phones.
- **What we sell:** Nothing. The guide is free, with no sign-up. The only conversion is a visit to the site.

## What we sound like

- **Language:** captions are English, written for Instagram search (owner's call, 7 Oct): the first line carries the
  search phrase (Durga Puja 2026, Kolkata, the area or topic), then a short body, one ask, the keywords in brackets
  and at most three hashtags (layout: marketing/captions.py). Bengali appears in a caption only as an occasional
  single line where it adds feeling, never in every post. Voiceovers and on-screen text stay Bengali-first.
- **Credits:** never in the caption. Photo and footage credits go on the media (corner of the photo, foot of the
  slide, the reel's end card).
- **Voice:** a warm, persuasive Bengali woman in her mid-twenties who has done every pujo night on foot. In
  voiceovers she is the ElevenLabs preset "Nadine"; when it is unavailable, edge-tts bn-IN-TanishaaNeural.
- **Words we use:** পুজো, ঠাকুর দেখা, প্যান্ডেল হপিং, মা, প্রতিমা, অঞ্জলি, ধুনুচি, পেটপুজো, pujo, pandal hopping, Maa,
  protima, the North Kolkata/South Kolkata split, Metro station names.
- **Words we never say:** "viral", "must-visit", "hidden gem", "stop scrolling", "you won't believe", "ultimate",
  "a feast for the senses", anything that sounds like a travel brochure.
- **Swearing:** no.
- **Emoji in captions:** two or three, as punctuation (🙏 💛 🥁 📻), never as the first character.
- **Face on camera:** faceless. Footage is pandal, idol, crowd, dhak, food; photos are credited CC/public-domain work.
- **Voiceover or to-camera:** voiceover.
- **Pace:** about 150 wpm for Bengali voiceover, slower than English; shots hold 5 seconds or more.

## Our positions

1. Pandal hopping randomly wastes the night: one area per night, starting from its Metro station, beats crossing
   the city.
2. The best time to see the famous pandals is the hour nobody goes, and that is data, not luck.
3. Bonedi bari pujos and the artisans of Kumartuli are as much pujo as the theme pandals.
4. Pujo is a ritual calendar (Mahalaya, Bodhon, Sandhi Puja, Sindoor Khela), not just five nights of crowds.

## Off limits

- Politics, religion-versus-religion comparisons, and anything about rival guides or apps.
- Crowd or safety claims we cannot back with the guide's own data.
- Other people's photos or videos without the creator's permission. Credit alone is not permission.
- Paid-promotion language: we have no sponsors.

## Proof we can use

- Live quiet hours for every pandal in the guide.
- Free, no sign-up, works on a phone.
- Pujo 2026 dates: Mahalaya Saturday 10 October; Shashthi to Dashami, 17 to 21 October.
