Where the skill and the house rules disagree, the house rules win (see [House rules](#house-rules-that-override-the-generic-skill)). 10 to 12 October are already planned in `marketing/daily/`, so this calendar starts on 13 October. ## Brand Context
- **Brand:** Pujo Parikrama 2026, a free Kolkata Durga Puja pandal-hopping guide: routes from the nearest Metro station and the hours each pandal is usually quiet. No sign-up, nothing to buy.
- **Audience:** Kolkata and Bengal pujo-goers aged 18 to 35 planning their nights, plus probashi Bengalis following from afar. Bengali at home, English on the phone.
- **Voice:** a warm Bengali woman in her mid-twenties who has done every pujo night on foot. Faceless; voiceover in spoken Kolkata Bengali. Captions in English for search, with the occasional Bengali line where it adds feeling (`marketing/voice.md`).
- **Positions:** one area per night, starting at its Metro station; the quiet hour is data, not luck; bonedi bari and Kumartuli are as much pujo as theme pandals; pujo is a ritual calendar, not just five nights of crowds.
- **Goal:** until Dashami, visits to the guide (tracked by `?src=ig_*`) and comments that start DMs. From 22 October, keep the audience through Lakshmi Puja, Kali Puja and Bhai Phonta for the festival guides and for Pujo 2027.
- **Differentiation:** sourced facts and honest estimates ("usually quiet", never "empty"), Metro-first routes, and rituals explained. No rival-bashing, no paid boosting, nothing sponsored.

## House rules that override the generic skill
| Generic skill says | Here we do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 5 to 15 hashtags | **At most 3**, at the end | Owner's call on 7 Oct (`marketing/voice.md`); Instagram now caps posts at 5 and treats tags as labels, not reach |
| Hook in the caption's first line | First line carries the **search phrase** (Durga Puja 2026, Kolkata, the area or ritual) in English | Instagram search reads captions; `marketing/caption_lint.py` checks it |
| "Tag someone who…" engagement bait | "Send this to…" or a real question | The humaniser (`ig-human`) flags tag-bait as a machine tell |
| Faces, day-in-the-life | Faceless: pandal, protima, crowd, dhak, food; voiceover | Brand rule; no founder name anywhere |
| Promotional = offers | Promotional = the free guide and tools | Nothing is sold; paid listings are off; no paid boosting (barter Collabs are fine) |
| Trending audio and clips | Only audio the app's library allows, only footage we own, Commons (credited) or creators' with written permission | Credit alone isn't permission |
| Links in captions | Never. Bio link, link stickers in Stories, or the PUJO keyword DM | Caption links aren't clickable |

Every caption below was run through `marketing/caption_lint.py` (search phrase, one ask, keyword line, at most 3 tags) and `.claude/skills/ig-human/detect.py` (all PASS). The post-pujo festival posts carry their own search phrase (Kali Puja, Bhai Phonta…) instead of "durga puja", on purpose.

**Schedule for any new source page:** day 1 publish the page and a Story with a link sticker; days 1 to 2 the carousel and the Facebook group posts; day 3 the Reel; day 5 a quiz Story; day 7 a different angle (a debate or a which-one-are-you); day 10 the engagement question; day 14 "in case you missed it" in Stories only (the feed never reposts the same carousel).

Other sources worth putting through this: the 6 ready-made routes (H3), the quiet-hour cards (H2), `/guide/metro/`, the Sandhi Puja section of `/guide/dates/`, `/guide/award-winning-pandals-2026/`, and each festival article from 24 October.

## Engagement Playbook
- **Questions:** every caption ends with exactly one ask. 24 of the 40 feed posts ask a question or invite a comment; the rest ask for a save, a send or the PUJO keyword.
- **Polls and quizzes:** in Stories every day of puja week, and at least twice a week after (see each day's Stories line).
- **Debates (1 to 2 a week):** Panchami vs Saptami (13 Oct), North vs South result (17 Oct), the juries' picks (26 Oct), pradip vs tuni (3 Nov).
- **Storytelling (1 to 2 a week):** dhakis who travel to play (14 Oct), Bodhon (17 Oct), one day on foot in North Kolkata (27 Oct), how the crowd model works (28 Oct).
- **Add Yours stickers:** Ashtami anjali (19 Oct), Lakshmi Puja alpana (25 Oct), Kali Puja lamps (8 Nov), Bhai Phonta plate (11 Nov). Reshare with credit; ask before featuring in the feed.
- **Comment-to-DM:** every PUJO comment gets the DM with `?src=ig_dm` and a public "DM-এ পাঠিয়ে দিলাম 💛". "Write your pandal" comments get its quiet hours from the app.
- **First hour after posting:** reply to every comment, ideally with a question back; pin the best one; repost the funniest answers to Stories, tagged.
- **Collabs:** invite the creators from `marketing/creators/` as Collab co-authors on the Reels marked with creator footage (barter only, no payment, no change to routes or pandal order).
