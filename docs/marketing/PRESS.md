## The story in one line

**A free web app that tells Kolkata's pandal hoppers when each pandal is usually quiet, so families can skip the
2-hour queue.** It covers 107 pandals, gives the metro or auto route and food nearby, and works in Bengali.

**Know the neighbour:** Kolkata Police's *Puja Bandhu* app (launched September 2025) shows live crowd counts and the
nearest metro. Journalists will ask. Our answer: we're complementary. Puja Bandhu tells you how crowded a pandal is
*now*; Pujo Parikrama helps you *plan ahead*: the usually-quiet hours for each day, a walking route across an area,
food on the way, in Bengali, with no install. Never knock it; it's the police's crowd-safety tool.

**Angles that fit what these desks ran last year:** (1) *beat the queue*: quiet hours at the famous pandals (every
outlet ran crowd and record-Metro-ridership stories in 2025); (2) *Metro-first pandal hopping*: pandals by station,
plus autos (Curly Tales ran "metro stations closest to iconic pandals" in 2025); (3) *Bengali-first, Kolkata-made and
free*, for Bengali dailies and TV, with food near each pandal for lifestyle desks.

**Claims we never make:** "empty", "no queue", "real-time crowd" before Shashthi, or user numbers we haven't
reached. Say "usually quiet" and "estimated".

## Press note (English, about 250 words)

**Skip the queue this pujo: a free Kolkata app shows when each of 107 pandals is usually quiet**

*Kolkata, [date].* Every October, lakhs of people queue for hours outside Kolkata's best-known pandals. Often a
pandal with a two-hour queue at 9 pm has hardly any queue at 7 in the morning. **Pujo Parikrama**
(pujoparikramaguide.in), a free web app made in Kolkata, puts that timing in everyone's pocket.

For each of 107 pandals, from Bagbazar and Kumartuli Park in the north to Sreebhumi, Tala Prattoy, Ekdalia
Evergreen and Behala's theme pujas, the app shows the hours it is usually quietest on each day of the puja. It also
shows the nearest metro station and the walk from it, the buses and shared autos that go there, and the food
within walking distance. A planner turns a few chosen areas into a timed walking route, and six ready-made trails
cover the city from North Kolkata's heritage pujas to an all-night North-to-South run.

The app works in Bengali, English and Hindi in any phone browser, with no download and no sign-up. From Shashthi,
visitors can check in at a pandal, which shows everyone which pandals are busy at that moment.

"Pujo is about the pandals, the people and the food, not the queue," said the Pujo Parikrama team. "If a family
knows Sreebhumi is calmer at dawn, they spend the evening somewhere else, and the crowd spreads out a little."

The 2026 puja runs from Shashthi on 17 October to Dashami on 21 October. Mahalaya is on 10 October.

**Link:** pujoparikramaguide.in · **Instagram:** @pujoparikrama.guide · **Contact:** pujoparikramaguide.in/contact/
