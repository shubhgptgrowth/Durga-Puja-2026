# Instagram content playbook (@pujoparikrama.guide)

**The bar for every post:** would a Bengali send it to their family group or their probashi cousin? If not, don't post it. Utility (routes, quiet hours) belongs in stories, the app and the daily kit card. The feed is for identity, nostalgia and debate.

## What makes pujo content get shared

1. **Bengali first.** Lead with Bengali script in calligraphy (Galada); put English second, smaller. Bengali text is a signal of belonging, and it's what probashi Bengalis forward.
2. **Culture, not logistics.** Words, rituals, food, childhood, para pride. Pandal lists and how-tos are useful but rarely shared.
3. **Real or clearly artistic visuals.** Use real pujo photos (Wikimedia Commons, credited) or Kalighat-pat-style illustrations, which are Kolkata's own folk art. No generic gradients, no stock-photo look, and no AI imagery that pretends to be real.
4. **One look.** The laal-paar border (red sari border, gold line), cream paper and maroon ink. Everything from `marketing/posts4/design.py` has it.
5. **A reason to comment on every post.** Comments and shares are what push a post to non-followers.

## Formats that drive comments

| Format | Example | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Fill in the blank | পুজো মানে ________ | Easy to answer, emotional, and the answers are content for stories |
| Pick a side | উত্তর না দক্ষিণ? | Identity, playful rivalry, one-word replies, people tag the other side |
| Which one are you? | 90s pujo: comment the number | Nostalgia plus a low-effort reply plus tagging |
| Bingo / checklist | Pujo bingo 2026 | Screenshot, tick and reshare to stories, which is free reach |
| Utility for a comment | "Comment your pandal, we reply with its quiet hours" | Every comment is a reply thread. Use comment-to-DM automation |
| Words / meanings | 9 words English can't translate | Saved and sent to people far from home |
| Quiz in stories | কলা বউ আসলে কে? | Story replies count as engagement |

**Hooks:** a question in Bengali, a strong opinion ("Chaturthi is the new Saptami"), a "POV", or "only Bengalis will get this". The first line of the caption is Bengali, a question, with the call to action in the first 125 characters.

**After posting:**
- Reply to every comment in the first hour, ideally with a question back.
- Pin the best comment.
- Repost the funniest answers to stories and tag the people who wrote them.
- For "Comment your pandal", reply with the pandal's quiet hours from the app (or set up ManyChat comment-to-DM).
- Count the উত্তর/দক্ষিণ votes and announce the winner on Shashthi, which gives you a second post.

## Cadence (5–21 Oct)

- **Feed: 2–3 a day.** One culture post (from this playbook), one reel (batch 2, 18:45 IST from 11 Oct), and the daily kit card (08:15 auto).
- **Stories: 5–8 a day.** A poll, quiz, slider or countdown every day, reposted comments, and a link sticker to the app.
- Best times for Kolkata: 12:30–13:30 and 19:30–22:30 IST. On puja days, also after midnight.
