**Goal:** 1,00,000 people reach Pujo Parikrama **before Shashthi (17 Oct)**, without paid ads. People who
reach it through the app and through the guide pages both count, once each (see §7).

**The diagnosis in one line: the product is ready, but almost nobody has heard of it.** The team has been
building faster than it has been telling people. The next 10 days have to reverse that ratio: about 80 %
distribution and 20 % product, and the product work should only be things that make sharing or search work better.

### What's strong (keep it)

* **A real utility nobody else has packaged:** quiet hours per pandal, metro/bus/auto routes, food nearby, free, no sign-up, in Bengali.
* **Search-ready:** about 400 crawlable pages, JSON-LD, sitemap, IndexNow and llms.txt. This is better SEO plumbing than most news sites have.
* **Share plumbing:** tracked links, deep links to a pandal, a WhatsApp button on every sheet, story cards.
* **A content engine** that can make Bengali-first posts and Reels every day.

### What's holding growth back

1. **No distribution owner or routine.** The daily routine in PLAN.md §4 has not been logged once.
2. **The Instagram account is new, so the algorithm shows it to almost nobody.** Posting about 20 Reels in one catch-up batch (4 Oct) to an account with no followers spreads thin reach even thinner. Own-account Instagram can't carry 1 lakh in 10 days. Other people's audiences (creators, press, groups) have to.
3. **Too many features compete for the headline.** Radio, dhak pads, badges, a step tracker, narration, photo stories, offers and parking are all in the shop window. A first-time visitor needs one reason to stay.
4. **Themes are missing**, and "theme" is the pujo word of October.
5. **Measurement is blind to search**, which makes SEO impossible to steer.

## 2. Positioning

**One promise:** ***ভিড় এড়িয়ে ঠাকুর দেখুন।*** *See every pandal. Skip the queue.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **For** | Kolkata families, college groups and probashi Bengalis coming home, planning pandal hopping |
| **Who are tired of** | 2-hour queues, zig-zagging across the city, and "kothay jabo aaj?" fights in the family group |
| **Pujo Parikrama is** | the free pujo planner that tells you **when** each pandal is quiet, **how** to get there by metro or auto, and **what** to eat next door |
| **Unlike** | listicles from news sites (no timing, no route) and Google Maps (no pujo knowledge) |
| **Proof** | 107 pandals, quiet hours hour by hour, a 3-tap route, works in Bengali, no sign-up |

**Rules that come with it**

* **Lead with the queue, never with features.** Every post, poster and press line opens with time saved or the queue skipped. The step tracker, badges, radio and narration stay in the app as delights. They never lead.
* **Honest about the model.** Before live check-ins, the crowd times are estimates. Say "usually quiet at 7 am", never "empty at 7 am". A promise broken in a queue on Ashtami kills word of mouth.
* **Voice:** a warm, witty Kolkata dada or didi. Bengali first, English second. The same "laal-paar" look as now.
* **One name everywhere:** Pujo Parikrama · pujoparikramaguide.in · @pujoparikrama.guide.

## 4. Channel plan: how we reach 1 lakh

The own Instagram account can't get there alone. The plan borrows audiences that already exist.

Pujo-utility stories get shared widely | 15–30k |

**The honest call:** 1 lakh before Shashthi is reachable only if **press and creators both land in the next 5 days.**
The central forecast is about 70k by Panchami and 1 lakh crossed on Saptami. Section 6 says what we change if a
weekly gate is missed.

### 4.1 Press (the biggest single lever, owner needed today)

* **Angle:** "Kolkata's queues, solved by a free app made by Kolkata people. It tells you the quiet hour for 107 pandals." Second angle: "UNESCO-heritage pujo gets its own open guide".
* **Kit (I'll make it):** a one-page press note in English and Bengali, 6 screenshots, a 30-second screen recording, founder quotes, and facts (pandals, trails, languages, free, no data collected).
* **Send:** 7–8 Oct to Kolkata desks and city-tech reporters. Follow up on 9 Oct with the Mahalaya hook. Re-pitch on 14 Oct with the "themes list" and live data.
* **Each outlet gets its own code** (`press_telegraph`…) so we can thank and re-pitch the ones that deliver.

### 4.2 Creators (owner needed today)

* **List of 40, aim for 15 yes.** Mix food (Kolkata food bloggers), city and travel, meme pages (Bong humour), and pujo/photography accounts. Mid-size accounts (20k–300k) say yes more often than the biggest ones.
* **Offer (no money):** a Collab post, their handle credited on our cards, early access to the themes list, a "creator's route" trail named after them in the app, and a feature in our stories.
* **The ask:** one Reel or story using the app on a real outing ("I skipped the Sreebhumi queue"), with a link sticker to `?src=creator_<handle>`.
* Every DM is tracked in TRACKER.md §3. Scripts are in PLAN.md §9, plus a shorter Bengali-first version I'll add.

### 4.3 Search (product-side, see §5)

* The themes page (H1), a theme line on every pandal page, "2026" in titles, `?src=seo_*` on every "Open app" link, and measurement on guide pages.
* **Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools today** if they aren't done yet (SEO.md "One-time" steps). Submit the sitemap and request indexing for the top 20 pages.
* Answer engines: keep IndexNow running after every theme update.

### 4.4 WhatsApp (everyone, 15 minutes a day)

* The Channel goes live today (it's still ⬜). One post a day plus live tips on puja days.
* **"Pujo para captains":** recruit 30 friends and family, each with their own code (`wa_<name>`), to forward the day's kit message to 10 groups. A leaderboard of their codes motivates them.
* RWAs and offices: send the RWA script (PLAN.md §9) to 50 housing societies and 20 office groups by 12 Oct.

### 4.5 Instagram (change of approach)

* **Cadence:** at most 2 feed posts a day (one Reel, one carousel), plus 5–8 stories. No more catch-up batches.
* **Formats that earn reach for a small account:** faces and voices (real people in queues, a "skip the queue" before/after), humour and debate (উত্তর না দক্ষিণ), daily theme reveals, and Collabs. How-to Reels about app features go to stories and Highlights, not the feed.
* **Comment-to-DM:** "Comment your pandal 👇 and we'll DM you its quiet hours". Every comment boosts reach and turns into a DM with a tracked link.
* **Every Reel** gets trending pujo audio, a Bengali hook in the first second, the location tag, and a Collab invite where possible.

### 4.6 Offline

* Committees and eateries: 20 posters by 14 Oct, starting with the 10 most-searched pandals (Bagbazar, Ekdalia, Deshapriya, Sreebhumi, Lebutala, Santosh Mitra Square, Kumartuli Park, Tala Prattoy, Suruchi, Chetla Agrani). The pitch: "We spread your crowd".
* Colleges: NSS and fest teams as "pandal ambassadors" with `college_<name>` codes.

## 5. Product roadmap for growth (until code freeze on 16 Oct)

**Feature freeze from today** on anything that isn't one of these. Narration, photo stories, radio, music, new
badges and new reels tooling wait until after Bijoya.

| # | Item | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Measure the guide pages:** a tiny beacon (same anonymous device id and `track_open`) on every guide page, and `?src=seo_<type>` on every "Open app" link | Without it, search is invisible and the 1-lakh count is wrong | 7 Oct |
| G2 | **2026 themes:** a `theme_2026` column, a theme line on each pandal sheet and page, the `/guide/themes-2026/` list page, and a daily theme carousel from the same data | H1. The biggest search and share opportunity | Page 8 Oct, filled daily from 10 Oct |
| G3 | **First screen for a newcomer:** one line with the promise, a "Plan my pujo" button, and "When is it quiet?" search. Hide empty states (0 check-ins, an empty Moments feed) until there's data | People who arrive from Instagram or WhatsApp decide in 3 seconds | 8 Oct |
| G4 | **"Send this plan" image:** a route or trail as a 1080×1350 image plus a link, made for the family group | H3. Each plan becomes a forward | 10 Oct |
| G5 | **Speed:** lazy-load the map, slim `guide.json`, defer anything not needed on first paint | Pandal-side 4G is slow, and a slow first open loses people | 12 Oct |
| G6 | **Add to home screen** after a saved plan or a first check-in | Repeat use over the 5 days | 14 Oct |
| G7 | **Capacity:** Supabase Pro (US$25) and Cloudflare proxied in front of Pages | 1 lakh devices break the free plan. P0-1 has waited since 3 Oct | **Before 10 Oct** |

## 7. How we count

* **One person = one device**, counted once in the app or on a guide page (G1 joins the two). We report people reached and new people per day, by source code.
* **Funnel KPIs:** opens → a pandal or plan viewed (activation) → a share or forward → a check-in (puja days).
* **Daily 21:00 report** (it already exists) plus a short note in TRACKER.md: yesterday's number, the top 3 sources, and what we change tomorrow.
* **Rule of thumb:** a channel earns tomorrow's hours with today's `?src=` numbers.
