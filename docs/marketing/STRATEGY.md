> **Status: draft for owner approval (6 Oct).** Written by the brand strategist (Claude) after reviewing the app, the
> guide pages, the Instagram pipeline, the reach reports and the backlog. Once approved, this file sets the

**Goal:** 1,00,000 people reach Pujo Parikrama **before Shashthi (17 Oct)**, without paid ads. People who
reach it through the app and through the guide pages both count, once each (see §7).

| Marketing P0 tasks done | 1 of 7 (Instagram account) | Committees, creators, the WhatsApp Channel, flyers and the Mahalaya push are all ⬜ |

**The diagnosis in one line: the product is ready, but almost nobody has heard of it.** The team has been
building faster than it has been telling people. The next 10 days have to reverse that ratio: about 80 %
distribution and 20 % product, and the product work should only be things that make sharing or search work better.

### What's strong (keep it)

* **A real utility nobody else has packaged:** quiet hours per pandal, metro/bus/auto routes, food nearby, free, no sign-up, in Bengali.
* **Search-ready:** about 400 crawlable pages, JSON-LD, sitemap, IndexNow and llms.txt. This is better SEO plumbing than most news sites have.
* **Share plumbing:** tracked links, deep links to a pandal, a WhatsApp button on every sheet, story cards.
* **A content engine** that can make Bengali-first posts and Reels every day.

### What's holding growth back

1. **No distribution owner or routine.** The daily routine in PLAN.md §4 has not been logged once.
2. **The Instagram account is new, so the algorithm shows it to almost nobody.** Posting about 20 Reels in one catch-up batch (4 Oct) to an account with no followers spreads thin reach even thinner. Own-account Instagram can't carry 1 lakh in 10 days. Other people's audiences (creators, press, groups) have to.
3. **Too many features compete for the headline.** Radio, dhak pads, badges, a step tracker, narration, photo stories, offers and parking are all in the shop window. A first-time visitor needs one reason to stay.
4. **Themes are missing**, and "theme" is the pujo word of October.
5. **Measurement is blind to search**, which makes SEO impossible to steer.

## 2. Positioning

**One promise:** ***ভিড় এড়িয়ে ঠাকুর দেখুন।*** *See every pandal. Skip the queue.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **For** | Kolkata families, college groups and probashi Bengalis coming home, planning pandal hopping |
| **Who are tired of** | 2-hour queues, zig-zagging across the city, and "kothay jabo aaj?" fights in the family group |
| **Pujo Parikrama is** | the free pujo planner that tells you **when** each pandal is quiet, **how** to get there by metro or auto, and **what** to eat next door |
| **Unlike** | listicles from news sites (no timing, no route) and Google Maps (no pujo knowledge) |
| **Proof** | 107 pandals, quiet hours hour by hour, a 3-tap route, works in Bengali, no sign-up |

**Rules that come with it**

* **Lead with the queue, never with features.** Every post, poster and press line opens with time saved or the queue skipped. The step tracker, badges, radio and narration stay in the app as delights. They never lead.
* **Honest about the model.** Before live check-ins, the crowd times are estimates. Say "usually quiet at 7 am", never "empty at 7 am". A promise broken in a queue on Ashtami kills word of mouth.
* **Voice:** a warm, witty Kolkata dada or didi. Bengali first, English second. The same "laal-paar" look as now.
* **One name everywhere:** Pujo Parikrama · pujoparikramaguide.in · @pujoparikrama.guide.

**The honest call:** 1 lakh before Shashthi is reachable only if **press, creators and WhatsApp all land in the next 5 days.**

### 4.1 Press (the biggest single lever, owner needed today)

* **Angle:** "Kolkata's queues, solved by a free app made by Kolkata people. It tells you the quiet hour for 107 pandals." Second angle: "UNESCO-heritage pujo gets its own open guide".
* **Kit (I'll make it):** a one-page press note in English and Bengali, 6 screenshots, a 30-second screen recording, founder quotes, and facts (pandals, trails, languages, free, no data collected).
* **Send:** 7–8 Oct to Kolkata desks and city-tech reporters. Follow up on 9 Oct with the Mahalaya hook. Re-pitch on 14 Oct with the "themes list" and live data.
* **Each outlet gets its own code** (`press_telegraph`…) so we can thank and re-pitch the ones that deliver.

### 4.2 Creators (owner needed today)

* **List of 40, aim for 15 yes.** Mix food (Kolkata food bloggers), city and travel, meme pages (Bong humour), and pujo/photography accounts. Mid-size accounts (20k–300k) say yes more often than the biggest ones.
* **Offer (no money):** a Collab post, their handle credited on our cards, early access to the themes list, a "creator's route" trail named after them in the app, and a feature in our stories.
* **The ask:** one Reel or story using the app on a real outing ("I skipped the Sreebhumi queue"), with a link sticker to `?src=creator_<handle>`.
* Every DM is tracked in TRACKER.md §3. Scripts are in PLAN.md §9, plus a shorter Bengali-first version I'll add.

### 4.3 Search (product-side, see §5)

* The themes page (H1), a theme line on every pandal page, "2026" in titles, `?src=seo_*` on every "Open app" link, and measurement on guide pages.
* **Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools today** if they aren't done yet (SEO.md "One-time" steps). Submit the sitemap and request indexing for the top 20 pages.
* Answer engines: keep IndexNow running after every theme update.

### 4.4 WhatsApp (everyone, 15 minutes a day)

* The Channel goes live today (it's still ⬜). One post a day plus live tips on puja days.
* **"Pujo para captains":** recruit 30 friends and family, each with their own code (`wa_<name>`), to forward the day's kit message to 10 groups. A leaderboard of their codes motivates them.
* RWAs and offices: send the RWA script (PLAN.md §9) to 50 housing societies and 20 office groups by 12 Oct.

### 4.5 Instagram (change of approach)

* **Cadence:** at most 2 feed posts a day (one Reel, one carousel), plus 5–8 stories. No more catch-up batches.
* **Formats that earn reach for a small account:** faces and voices (real people in queues, a "skip the queue" before/after), humour and debate (উত্তর না দক্ষিণ), daily theme reveals, and Collabs. How-to Reels about app features go to stories and Highlights, not the feed.
* **Comment-to-DM:** "Comment your pandal 👇 and we'll DM you its quiet hours". Every comment boosts reach and turns into a DM with a tracked link.
* **Every Reel** gets trending pujo audio, a Bengali hook in the first second, the location tag, and a Collab invite where possible.

### 4.6 Offline

* Committees and eateries: 20 posters by 14 Oct, starting with the 10 most-searched pandals (Bagbazar, Ekdalia, Deshapriya, Sreebhumi, Lebutala, Santosh Mitra Square, Kumartuli Park, Tala Prattoy, Suruchi, Chetla Agrani). The pitch: "We spread your crowd".
* Colleges: NSS and fest teams as "pandal ambassadors" with `college_<name>` codes.

## 7. How we count

* **One person = one device**, counted once in the app or on a guide page (G1 joins the two). We report people reached and new people per day, by source code.
* **Funnel KPIs:** opens → a pandal or plan viewed (activation) → a share or forward → a check-in (puja days).
* **Daily 21:00 report** (it already exists) plus a short note in TRACKER.md: yesterday's number, the top 3 sources, and what we change tomorrow.
* **Rule of thumb:** a channel earns tomorrow's hours with today's `?src=` numbers.

## 8. Audit notes (6 Oct)

Both audits ran on a local build at iPhone 13 size (the live site isn't reachable from the audit machine).
The screenshots are kept with the session, not in the repo.

### 8.1 App UX: what costs us first-time visitors and shares

| # | Finding | Fix | Goes into |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The first tap anywhere starts the dhak** (`radioCard.js` `armAutoplay`), and a toast covers the list. A visitor from Instagram on a bus gets drums | No autoplay. Sound only when the radio is tapped. Don't preload `dhak.mp3` (1.75 MB) | G3, **day 1** |
| 2 | **Text that can't be seen:** `.pad` is defined twice in `styles.css` (lines 87 and 560). The radio's button style overrides the page-padding class used about 18 times, so some empty states and hints are cream on cream | Rename the radio class (e.g. `.tap-pad`) | G3, **day 1** |
| 3 | **The first screen doesn't say what the app does.** Above the fold: a greeting, "Ma ashchhen!", the Pujo Radio card. "Plan your route" is a list row low on the screen | One promise line in the hero ("107 pandals · when each is quiet · metro & auto routes · free"), one solid "Plan my pujo →" button, the radio card below the fold | G3 |
| 4 | **The best planning content is buried:** the 6-day plan and the trails are about 3,200 px down Home. Plan opens on a wizard whose Next button stays disabled | Plan opens with "Pick your day → here's your route" (day plans and trails first, custom route second) | G3 / G4 |
| 5 | **Shared links all look the same in WhatsApp.** Shares are `?src=…#p=<id>`. The `#p=` part is invisible to link previews, so every forward shows the generic text-on-red `og.png` | Share `/guide/pandals/<id>/` links (they open the app), a per-pandal preview image (photo, name, quiet hours), and a new `og.png` with a protima photo | G4 |
| 6 | **The floating dhak and shankh buttons cover content** on every tab (step 3 on Home, the hint on Plan, Me, Explore cards) | Remove them. The header dhak already exists | G3 |
| 7 | **Empty states before the pujas:** a wall of zeros on Me, "0 visits / 0 today" on sheets, a "Live" pill with no number | Hide counters under a threshold. Before Shashthi, Me becomes "Your pujo starts in N days: pick your days". Hide the pill until it has a number | G3 |
| 8 | **The share loop is hard to find.** "Share my Pujo card" is low on Me, under a phone-number field that reads like data collection | Make the phone field optional and collapsed. "Share this plan" on Plan results and the 6-day plan. "Send to your pujo gang" on Home | G4 |
| 9 | **No install prompt**, and Instagram's in-app browser can't install anyway | Detect the Instagram/Facebook in-app browser and suggest "Open in Chrome/Safari". Offer install after a saved plan or first check-in ("works offline in the crowd") | G6 |
| 10 | **Bengali polish:** letter-spacing breaks the joined letters in the hero subtitle; digits are mixed (৫ next to 5); descriptions stay English | `letter-spacing: 0` under `:lang(bn)`, one digit style, then translate the top-40 highlights | G3 |

**Speed (cold load on a slow 4G, CPU slowed 4×):**
* The hero appears at about 8.5 s, and the largest image at about 15 s. On GitHub Pages with compression, the hero should come in at about 4–5 s.
* The cause is images. All 5 hero slides load at once (about 980 KB).
* Hindi strings (58 KB) and the map libraries (270 KB) load on Home for everyone.
* **Fix (G5):** WebP heroes at about 60 KB each. Load slides 2–5 after the first paint. Load the map and Hindi only when they're needed.

**Genuinely good:**
* The pandal sheet (sticky Directions + WhatsApp Share, crowd by hour, honest "≈ estimate" labels, metro walk times, food nearby).
* The Explore list.
* The visual identity.
* The Bengali fonts.
* The Moments empty state ("be the first", plus the archive).

### 8.2 Search: what the guide pages need

**Inventory:** 439 URLs:
* 107 pandal pages
* 175 eatery pages
* 13 area pages and 6 trails
* 50 knowledge articles (rituals, recipes)
* 14 Navratri and festival pages
* 64 Web Stories

**Done right:**
* Clean canonicals on the domain, nothing wrongly noindexed, valid JSON-LD everywhere, light pages.
* robots.txt welcomes crawlers, including AI ones. IndexNow is wired in.

| # | Fix | Why |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Verify Google Search Console and Bing today**, submit the sitemap and request indexing for about 10 key pages | Nothing below matters until Google knows the site exists. `site.json` has no verification tag, and nothing records a DNS verification |
| S2 | **2026 themes:** a `theme_2026` column flowing into each pandal page's title, H1, lead and an FAQ, plus the `/guide/themes-2026/` list page (H1 in §3) | "<pandal> theme 2026" is the long tail a new domain can win |
| S3 | **Shorter titles aimed at what people type:** 390 of 440 titles are over 65 characters. Pandal titles become "<Name> Durga Puja 2026: Theme, Best Time, Metro" | Click-through and relevance |
| S4 | **Measure:** a beacon on guide pages, `?src=seo_<type>` on every "Open app" link, and a CTA on knowledge articles (they have none) | G1. Search is invisible today |
| S5 | **List pages:** "Best pandals in Kolkata 2026" (top 25 with themes) and one page per region (North, South…). Retitle /guide/ to "Kolkata Durga Puja Pandal List 2026" | They match the list-style searches |
| S6 | **A real dates page:** tithi start and end times, Sandhi Puja and anjali times from one named panjika, plus Metro puja timings (a new page, updated the moment Metro Railway announces) | "date and time" and "metro timing" spike every puja night |
| S7 | **Bengali pages** for dates and the anjali mantra, with hreflang | Bengali results are less crowded with strong pages |
| S8 | **Eatery pages:** take the 175 template-heavy pages out of the sitemap until after the pujas, and fold the best eateries into the area pages | Thin pages drag down a new domain |
| S9 | **Timely Web Stories:** "Theme reveals 2026", "Top pandals 2026", one per puja day, with the year in each title | The 64 existing stories are evergreen |
| S10 | One H1 on the app home page (it has two) | Hygiene |

**Expectation, so nobody is surprised:**
* On a new domain, Google brings roughly **300–2,000 clicks before Dashami**, almost all long tail, mostly 17–21 Oct.
* Head terms belong to the big news sites, Drik Panchang and travel portals.
* Bing, ChatGPT and Copilot (through IndexNow) are worth tens to low hundreds.
* Search is worth doing because it is cheap, and because press and forwards will land on these pages. It won't carry the goal.
