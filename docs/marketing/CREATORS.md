# Creator collabs: who to ask, what to send (8–14 Oct)

> **Marketing track**, STRATEGY.md §4.2. Unpaid only: barter and credit, no money, no boosts (owner decision 6).
> Send from @pujoparikrama.guide. Instagram's API can't send a first message to someone who hasn't messaged us, so
> these go by hand. That's about 20 minutes for the whole list.

**Why this matters:**
* Five creators posting one Collab Reel each reach more Kolkata phones in a day than our own account does in a month.
* Each has a personal link code, `creator_<handle>`. The 21:00 reach report shows exactly who delivered.

## What we offer (and can actually deliver)

1. **A ready-made hook for their content:** the page that fits their niche.
   * Food creators: food near each pandal and late-night food.
   * Photographers: the 2026 themes list and the quiet hours, for clean frames.
   * Pujo and city pages: pandals by metro station.
2. **Their own link**, which shows them how many people they sent. We send the number after the pujas.
3. **Credit:** their Reel in our stories, a mention in our daily card, and Collab on any post that uses their footage
   (with permission, see [../../marketing/creators/README.md](../../marketing/creators/README.md)).

Don't promise anything else: no app features named after them, no paid posts.

## The messages

Send in the creator's language: Bengali first for Bengali-language creators, English for the rest. Change `{handle}`
and `{link}` from the table. Open with something specific about one of their posts. That one line decides whether they
reply.

**Food creators**

> নমস্কার! আপনার {one dish/place from their recent post}-এর রিলটা দারুণ 🙏 আমরা @pujoparikrama.guide, কলকাতার ঠাকুর দেখার
> একটা ফ্রি গাইড: কোন প্যান্ডেলে কখন ভিড় কম, পাশে কী খাবেন, আর রাত বারোটার পরে কোথায় খাবার পাবেন।
> এবারের পুজোয় একটা "ঠাকুর দেখা + খাওয়া" রিল করবেন? আমাদের স্টোরি আর ডেইলি কার্ডে আপনার নাম থাকবে, আর পুজোর পরে
> জানাব আপনার লিংক থেকে কতজন এসেছেন। আপনার লিংক: {link}

## When someone says yes

1. Send them their area's station page and the late-night page, and offer to plan their route.
2. When their reel is up:
   * reshare it to our story the same hour, with credit and their link sticker;
   * add a line to that day's kit card.
3. If we want to use their footage in our own reel, follow the permission steps in `marketing/creators/README.md`.
4. After Bijoya, send them their numbers from the reach report (`creator_<handle>`).
