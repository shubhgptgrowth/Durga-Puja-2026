# Community posts: ready to paste (8–16 Oct)

> **Marketing track.** Part of [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md) §4.4 (WhatsApp) and the "Communities" row of §4.
> Search can't carry a new domain before Shashthi, so traffic comes from people passing the link on. These are the
> posts, who sends them and when. Every link carries its own `?src=` code, so the reach report (21:00 IST daily)
> shows which post worked. Brand voice: "we", no founder name (owner decision 5).

**Where we started (6 Oct reach report):**
* About 170 devices have opened the app.
* Where they came from: direct 65, personal WhatsApp 62, Instagram bio 34.
* WhatsApp already works. These posts scale it, and add Reddit and Facebook groups, where Kolkata asks "which pandals
  should I see?" every year.

**The hook for every post is something useful, not the app:**
* Pandals near your metro station: [/guide/metro/](https://pujoparikramaguide.in/guide/metro/).
* The 2026 themes list.
* The quiet hours.

People forward a list; nobody forwards an ad.

## 1. Reddit, r/kolkata (owner's own account, once, 8 or 9 Oct, 7–9 pm)

Read the sidebar rules first. If self-promotion needs mod approval, message the mods with the post text before posting.
Reply to every comment within the hour on the first evening. That's what keeps a post on the front page.

**Title:** Made a free list of which pujo pandals are near each metro station, with the quiet hours for each (2026)

**Body:**

> Pandal hopping plans in every family group come down to the same three questions: which pandals are close
> together, how to get there, and when they're less crowded. So we put the answers in one place.
>
> * **Pandals by metro station:** for each station, which pandals are within a 20-minute walk, nearest first.
>   Girish Park has Simla Byayam Samity (9 min), Chorbagan, Chaltabagan and Kashi Bose Lane all within 18 minutes.
>   https://pujoparikramaguide.in/guide/metro/?src=reddit_kolkata
> * **When each pandal is usually quiet**, day by day. These are estimates from a crowd model, not live counts.
>   During the pujas, people's check-ins show which pandals are busy right now.
> * **2026 themes** as committees announce them.
>
> It's free, no sign-up, works in Bengali. If a pin is wrong or your para's puja is missing, tell me here and I'll fix
> it tonight. Also curious: which station do you start from?

Then edit in fixes people suggest ("Edit: added X, thanks u/…"). That's the best follow-up there is.

**r/bengali and r/india:** don't post there. They're not the audience, and cross-posting reads as spam.

## 2. Facebook groups (owner and team, 8–14 Oct, 2–3 groups a day each)

Good fits are neighbourhood groups (Behala, Salt Lake, Dum Dum, Jadavpur, Howrah), Kolkata food groups, and
"Kolkata Durga Puja" photo groups. Post only where the rules allow links; otherwise put the link in the first comment.
Code: `fb_<group>`, for example `fb_saltlake_residents`.

**Bengali:**

> এবার ঠাকুর দেখার প্ল্যান করতে গিয়ে একটা জিনিস কাজে লাগল: কোন মেট্রো স্টেশন থেকে কোন প্যান্ডেল হেঁটে কত মিনিট,
> আর কোন প্যান্ডেলে কখন ভিড় কম। {area}-এর জন্য: {two pandals near that area's station, with minutes}.
> পুরো লিস্ট: pujoparikramaguide.in/guide/metro/?src=fb_{group}
> ফ্রি, সাইন-আপ লাগে না। কোনও প্যান্ডেল বাদ পড়লে কমেন্টে লিখুন, যোগ করে দেব 🙏

**English:**

> Planning pandal hopping by metro this year? Here's which pandals are walkable from each station, nearest first,
> with the hours each one is usually quiet. For {area}: {two pandals with minutes}.
> Full list: pujoparikramaguide.in/guide/metro/?src=fb_{group}. Free, no sign-up. Tell us if your para's puja is missing.

Fill `{area}` from the group's neighbourhood using the station pages, e.g. Behala: "Behala Bazar → Behala Notun Dal
(2 min walk)". A local detail is what stops the post looking like spam.

## 3. Quora (team, one answer a day, 8–16 Oct)

Answer questions that already rank on Google, so the answer keeps sending people after the pujas too:
* "Which are the best Durga Puja pandals in Kolkata?"
* "What is the best way to go pandal hopping in Kolkata?"
* "How to avoid crowds during Durga Puja in Kolkata?"

Write a genuinely useful 150–250 word answer:
* name 5–8 pandals and their nearest stations;
* give the go-early advice;
* put one link at the end (`quora`).

Never paste the same answer twice; Quora collapses duplicates.

## 4. WhatsApp: housing societies, offices, colleges, family (everyone, daily)

The biggest channel we already have.
* Each person forwards to **10 groups a day** with their own code: `wa_<name>` for friends and family, `rwa_<society>`,
  `office_<company>`, `college_<name>`.
* The reach report ranks the codes. Share the top 5 in the team group every night.

**Housing society / RWA (Bengali first):**

**Family group, the night before each puja day (from 15 Oct):**

Take the hours from the pandal's page. Say "usually" or "estimated"; never "empty" or "no queue".

## 5. When Metro announces the puja-night timings (expected 12–15 Oct)

This is the single biggest search and forward moment before the pujas. Every desk runs it, and every family group asks.
