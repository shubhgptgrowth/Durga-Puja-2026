Privacy policy

Pujo Parikrama: Kolkata Durga Puja 2026 guide · Last updated 6 October 2026

Pujo Parikrama is a free guide to Kolkata's Durga Puja: pandals, food, parking, routes and a step counter. It works without an account, and most of it works offline. This page explains what we store, why, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • We don't sell your data or share your name, number, location or progress with anyone for advertising. Some guide pages may show Google ads (see Advertising and cookies); the app doesn't.
  • Your GPS position is used on your phone to check that you're at a pandal or eatery. The position itself is never stored.
  • Your name and phone number are only stored if you choose to save them, and you can remove them any time.
  • My Pujo → Settings → Delete all my data erases everything we hold about you, including your account.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's pages.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google Maps directions also open in Google's own app or site when you tap Directions.
  • When you use them, your browser loads content directly from these services: YouTube (Pujo Radio and Pujo Reels), Instagram and Facebook (a Pujo Reel loads their player only when you tap it), OpenStreetMap / CARTO (map tiles), Wikimedia Commons (pandal photos) and Google Fonts. Their own privacy policies apply.

Advertising and cookies

Some pages of the guide may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies (or similar identifiers) to show ads and to measure them, which can include ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, and learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. The app itself does not show ads, and we never share your name, number, location or progress with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it, or until the guide is retired after the 2026 festival season, whichever is first. Place totals (for example "1,200 visits today") are anonymous counts and stay after you delete your data.

Your choices

  • Delete all my data (My Pujo → Settings) erases your progress backup, name and number, ratings, likes, check-ins, posted photos and videos, any offers you submitted, and your account (guest or Google), as well as everything on your phone.
  • Remove my number (My Pujo) erases your phone number at once.
  • Sign out forgets your progress on that phone. It stays in your Google account until you delete it.
  • You can delete any photo or video you posted from its page in Moments.
  • Can't open the app any more, or want something else removed? Email us (see Contact below).
  • You can use the guide without signing in, without saving a number, and without posting anything.

Contact

Children

The guide is for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. Please don't post photos of other people's children without their parents' permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the guide will link to the new version.
