The short answer: **buy the Forum for Durgotsab Pujor Passport (₹999)** unless you cannot collect its booklet in person on 12 to 14 October. Its exclusive pandals are the heavyweight ones, and the ₹49 difference does not matter. If you plan to hop across the whole city on several nights, buy both: ₹1,949 gets you VIP entry at 75 different pandals.

> **Note:** These details were gathered on 10 October 2026 from the passes' own listings and a published comparison of their pandal lists (durgapujopandals.com, 4 October 2026). Committees sometimes join or leave a pass after launch, so check the official page on District or BookMyShow before you buy. We are not connected to either pass.

For 2026 the main puja days run from Shashthi, Saturday 17 October, to Bijoya Dashami, Wednesday 21 October; see the full [2026 dates](/guide/dates/). Both passes start on 15 October, so you can use them on Chaturthi and Panchami too, when many big pandals are already open and the crowds are thinner.

## How much the two lists overlap

The two passes share **31 pandals**. Another **23 are only on the Porikroma card** and **21 are only on the Pujor Passport**, which makes **75 different pandals** if you hold both.

That overlap is why the choice comes down to the exclusives: the 31 shared pandals are covered whichever pass you buy.

## Pandals only on the Pujor Passport (21)

These carry the Passport's case. Several of them draw some of the longest queues in the city.

## Big pandals that are on neither pass

Some of the most famous pujas sell no VIP entry through either pass. Everyone joins the same queue, so timing is everything: go early in the morning, about **7 to 11 am**, or after about **1:30 am**, until around 4:30 am.

- [Sreebhumi Sporting Club](/guide/pandals/sreebhumi/) and [Tala Prattoy](/guide/pandals/tala_prattoy/) in the north and north-east
- [College Square](/guide/pandals/college_square/) and [Mohammad Ali Park](/guide/pandals/md_ali_park/) in central Kolkata
- [Suruchi Sangha](/guide/pandals/suruchi_sangha/), [Ekdalia Evergreen](/guide/pandals/ekdalia/), [Singhi Park](/guide/pandals/singhi_park/), [Tridhara Sammilani](/guide/pandals/tridhara/), [Chetla Agrani](/guide/pandals/chetla_agrani/) and [Deshapriya Park](/guide/pandals/deshapriya_park/) in the south

## Which pass is right for you

**Buy the Pujor Passport** if:

- someone in your group can reach Sovabazar or Kalighat between 12 and 14 October, 12 noon to 8 pm, to collect the booklet;
- your route takes in North Kolkata's big names (Hatibagan, Kumartuli, Kashi Bose Lane) or the Ballygunge and Kalighat cluster;
- you want the round-the-clock window for after-midnight hopping.

**Buy the Porikroma card instead** if:

- nobody can collect the Passport booklet in person on 12 to 14 October (Porikroma can be home-delivered);
- you want the very early hours after Navami night: it runs until 5 am on 21 October, so check whether the Passport's last day ends at midnight on the 20th;
- most of your nights will be in Salt Lake, New Town, Ultadanga or Kankurgachi, where many of its exclusives are.

**Buy both** (₹1,949 for 75 pandals) if you are doing heavy pandal hopping across the city over several nights.

> **Tip:** A VIP pass only gets you through the gate at the pandal itself. The barricaded approach lanes around the biggest pujas still fill up on Saptami, Ashtami and Navami evenings. Take the Metro, cover one area per night, and keep the pass, the booklet and your group of four together.

## Before you go

- [ ] Book on the official app or site, District for Porikroma and BookMyShow for the Pujor Passport, rather than buying from a reseller.
- [ ] For the Pujor Passport, put the collection trip in your calendar: 12 to 14 October, 12 noon to 8 pm.
- [ ] Count your group: four people per pass, and on the Passport children five and over count.
- [ ] Plan routes by area, with the shared and exclusive lists above.
- [ ] For pandals on neither pass, plan an early-morning or after-midnight visit.
