Durga Puja is over, but Bengal's festival year isn't. These are the dates of the big festivals still to come in 2026 and all through 2027, calculated for **Kolkata**. They follow the lunar calendar, so the tithi can start or end at an odd hour, and calendars sometimes disagree by a day. Treat this page as a planner, and confirm with your panjika or your family priest before the day.

## The rest of 2026

- **Kali Puja and Diwali: Sunday 8 November.** Bengal worships Kali on the new moon of Kartik, usually through the night, while much of India worships Lakshmi. Bhoot Chaturdashi, with its fourteen lamps and fourteen greens, is the evening before. Read more: [Kali Puja and Diwali](/festivals/kali-puja-diwali/).
- **Bhai Phonta: Wednesday 11 November.** Sisters put the phonta on their brothers' foreheads. See [Bhai Phonta](/festivals/bhai-phonta/).
- **Jagaddhatri Puja: Thursday 19 November (Navami).** That is Drik Panchang's date for Kolkata; some calendars give 18 November. Chandannagar's lights and Krishnanagar's processions run over several days around it. See [Jagaddhatri Puja](/festivals/jagaddhatri-puja-chandannagar/).

## 2027

- **Saraswati Puja: Thursday 11 February.** Vasant Panchami, a morning puja, and the day every Bengali student remembers.
- **Durga Puja: Saptami on Wednesday 6 October.** Shashthi and the bodhon are on Tuesday 5 October. Pujo 2027 comes **twelve days earlier** than 2026, when Saptami was on 18 October. Calendars disagree on the later days in 2027, so check the panjika for Ashtami, Navami and Dashami before you book anything.
- **Kali Puja: Thursday 28 October**, with Lakshmi Puja for Diwali on Friday 29 October.
- **Bhai Phonta: Sunday 31 October.**
- **Jagaddhatri Puja: Sunday 7 November.**

We'll add more dates, such as Poila Baisakh and Rath Yatra, once they are confirmed. For how the season fits together, see [Bengal's festival season](/festivals/).
